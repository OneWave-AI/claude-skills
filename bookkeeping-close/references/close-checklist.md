# Month-End Close Checklist (SMB)

Work top to bottom. Order matters: cash first because every later step depends on complete transactions, balance sheet before P&L because a wrong balance sheet always means a wrong P&L. Mark each item done, not done (with reason), or not applicable.

## Contents
1. Cutoff and completeness
2. Cash and card reconciliations
3. Receivables and revenue
4. Payables and expenses
5. Payroll
6. Accruals, prepaids, deferrals
7. Fixed assets and loans

10. Sign-off and lock

## 1. Cutoff and completeness
- [ ] All bank and card feeds imported through the period end date; no feed disconnected mid-month (a gap shows as a statement that does not roll forward).
- [ ] Every item in the platform's review queue (QBO For Review, Xero unreconciled lines) through period end is matched, added, or explained.
- [ ] Sales invoices for work delivered in the period are issued and dated in the period.
- [ ] Vendor bills received for the period are entered with the bill date, not the payment date (accrual basis).
- [ ] Expense reports and reimbursements for the period are submitted.

## 2. Cash and card reconciliations
Reconcile every balance-sheet account that has a statement, not only the main checking account.
- [ ] Each checking and savings account reconciled to the statement ending balance, unexplained variance 0.00.
- [ ] Each credit card reconciled to its statement closing balance. Card statement periods rarely end on the month end; reconcile to the statement date, then confirm activity to month end is recorded.
- [ ] Payment processor clearing accounts (card processor, marketplace, payment app) reconciled: gross sales minus fees minus refunds equals payouts. Record processor fees as expense, not net deposits as revenue.
- [ ] Undeposited Funds (QBO) is empty or holds only payments actually received after the last deposit.
- [ ] Transfers between own accounts net to zero across the pair of accounts.
- [ ] Outstanding items older than 90 days listed for the accountant.
- [ ] Reconciliation reports saved (QBO: Reconcile > History by account; Xero: Bank Reconciliation Summary).

## 3. Receivables and revenue
- [ ] AR aging total equals the Accounts Receivable balance on the balance sheet.
- [ ] Customer payments applied to invoices, not left as unapplied credits.
- [ ] Deposits and retainers received before work is done recorded as a liability (customer deposits / deferred revenue), not revenue. Flag the treatment for the accountant if unsure.
- [ ] Doubtful accounts flagged; write-offs require owner approval.
- [ ] Sales tax collected recorded to the liability, not revenue.

## 4. Payables and expenses
- [ ] AP aging total equals Accounts Payable on the balance sheet.
- [ ] Bill payments applied to bills; no bill paid twice (once as an expense from the feed, once as a bill payment).
- [ ] Credit card charges categorized; receipts attached for items above the business's documentation threshold.
- [ ] Recurring vendors present (rent, software, insurance, utilities). A missing recurring charge is a completeness check.

## 5. Payroll
- [ ] Payroll expense per the payroll provider's register equals gross wages plus employer taxes in the ledger.
- [ ] Payroll liability accounts tie to amounts due per the provider; clearing accounts are zero after the net pay and tax debits post.
- [ ] Owner compensation classified consistently (wages vs draws/distributions depends on entity type: accountant question).
- [ ] Contractor payments tagged for year-end information reporting.

## 6. Accruals, prepaids, deferrals (accrual basis)
Skip on cash basis, but say that the books are cash basis in the package.
- [ ] Expenses incurred but not yet billed accrued (utilities, professional fees, commissions, wages earned after the last pay date).
- [ ] Prepaid expenses (annual insurance, annual software) amortized for the month.
- [ ] Deferred revenue recognized as earned.
- [ ] Prior-month accruals reversed so the actual bill does not double the expense.
All of these are proposed entries for accountant review, not posted by this skill.

## 7. Fixed assets and loans
- [ ] Purchases above the business's capitalization threshold flagged (capitalize vs expense is an accountant decision).
- [ ] Depreciation entry posted if the accountant provides a schedule.
- [ ] Each loan balance reconciled to the lender statement; payments split into principal (liability) and interest (expense). Booking the full payment as expense is a frequent error.
- [ ] Vehicle and equipment financing treated the same way.

## 8. Balance sheet review
Read every line. Each balance should be explainable in one sentence.
- [ ] No negative balances in asset accounts, no debit balances in liability accounts without an explanation.
- [ ] Opening Balance Equity is zero or explained (a non-zero balance usually means a setup error).
- [ ] Suspense, Uncategorized Asset, Ask My Accountant: zero or on the accountant list.
- [ ] Owner equity, contributions, and draws recorded to equity, not income or expense.
- [ ] Retained earnings unchanged from the prior close unless the accountant booked an entry (a change means someone edited a closed period).

## 9. P&L review
- [ ] Compare to prior month and same month prior year. Investigate lines that moved more than the business's threshold (default: 20% and a meaningful dollar amount).
- [ ] Uncategorized Income and Uncategorized Expense are zero.
- [ ] No transfers, loan proceeds, owner contributions, or card payments in revenue or expense.
- [ ] Gross margin in its normal range; a jump often means cost of goods was miscategorized.

## 10. Sign-off and lock
- [ ] Close package produced (see SKILL.md output section).
- [ ] Proposed adjusting entries approved and posted by the accountant or owner.
- [ ] Period locked after sign-off: QBO Settings > Account and settings > Advanced > Close the books (closing date, optional password); Xero lock dates (set by an adviser role). Locking protects reconciled periods from edits that later break the beginning balance.
- [ ] Management reports distributed.
