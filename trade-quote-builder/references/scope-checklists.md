# Scope Checklists: Commonly Forgotten Items

Walk the list for the trade before pricing. Each item ends up in one of two places: a priced line, or a written exclusion. The script's `trade` field turns on a keyword scan for the items marked with an asterisk (*), so if a starred item is neither priced nor excluded it appears as a `[CHECK]` line.

## Every trade

- Permit and inspection fees, plus the labor to pull the permit and meet the inspector *
- Mobilization: truck, fuel, drive time, parking, tolls, per crew day *
- Disposal: dumpster, haul-away, recycling or landfill fees, hazardous-material handling *
- Daily and final cleanup *
- Site protection: floor runners, drop cloths, dust walls, plastic
- Access: ladders, lifts, scaffold, attic or crawlspace time, stairs-only buildings, after-hours work
- Waste factor on every cut or bulk material; pack-size rounding (you buy whole boxes)
- Labor burden on every hour (payroll taxes, workers comp, liability, benefits, paid time off)
- Supervision, estimating time, and return trips for punch-list or inspection corrections
- Warranty reserve for callbacks
- Contingency for hidden conditions, or an explicit exclusion of them
- Material price escalation if the job starts weeks after the quote date (short validity window)
- Card processing fees, financing fees, retainage (commercial) and the cost of carrying it

## HVAC

- Refrigerant recovery from the old system *
- Line set replacement or flush, and the brazing consumables *
- Condensate drain, trap, pump, and float safety switch *
- Electrical disconnect and whip; breaker size match to the new equipment *
- Equipment pad or wall stand, hurricane or seismic straps where required *
- Plenum and duct transitions to the new cabinet size *
- Thermostat and control wire *
- Start-up, charge verification, airflow check, commissioning *
- Venting changes (80% to 90%+ furnace needs PVC venting and condensate), gas line work or capping
- Crane or extra crew for rooftop or attic equipment
- Load calculation if sizing is changing
- Equipment registration for extended manufacturer warranty

## Plumbing

- Shutoff valves and supply lines *
- Fittings, which run far above the pipe cost on short runs *
- Access openings and the drywall or tile patch afterward, or an exclusion *
- Code-triggered upgrades: expansion tank, pan and drain, venting, backflow, seismic straps *
- Pressure test and inspection *
- Water heater: venting, gas flex, sediment trap, haul-away of the old tank
- Excavation, concrete cutting, and restoration for underground or slab work
- Fixture trim if the client supplies fixtures (and who warranties them)

## Electrical

- Panel capacity and load calculation; service upgrade if needed *
- Utility coordination for disconnect and reconnect *
- Patch and repair of walls and ceilings after fishing wire, or an exclusion *
- Boxes, devices, cover plates, connectors, not only the wire *
- Inspection and corrections *
- Arc-fault and ground-fault protection now required by code in many locations
- Trenching and conduit for detached structures
- Surge protection, labeling the panel schedule

## Roofing

- Tear-off per layer; more than one layer changes labor and disposal *
- Decking or sheathing replacement allowance per sheet *
- Flashing: step, counter, chimney, skylight, wall *
- Drip edge *
- Underlayment and ice barrier where required *
- Ventilation: ridge vent, intake, pipe boots *
- Dumpster and magnetic nail sweep
- Steep-pitch and multi-story safety equipment and labor factor
- Gutter removal and reinstall

## Landscaping and hardscape

- Utility locate before digging (call the local locate service) *
- Grading and drainage *
- Irrigation adjustment or repair after the work *
- Delivery fees for soil, stone, and plants *
- Plant warranty terms and the replacement reserve *
- Soil amendment, fabric, edging, base compaction
- Equipment rental and trailer transport
- Spoils haul-off

## Painting

- Surface prep: patching, sanding, caulking, scraping *
- Primer or spot prime on patches, stains, and color changes *
- Masking and protection *
- Furniture moving and reset *
- Touch-up walkthrough *
- Coverage math: area x coats / spread rate, then waste, then round up to whole cans
- Lead-safe work practices for pre-1978 homes in the US (EPA RRP rule), which change labor and materials
- Ladders, scaffold, or lifts for high walls; stairwells
- Color count: each extra color adds cut-in labor

## Cleaning (commercial and residential)

- Supplies and consumables, and who provides them *
- Access: keys, alarm codes, after-hours entry *
- Frequency and visit count per month (quote per visit and per month) *
- Travel time between sites
- Specialty tasks priced separately: carpet extraction, floor strip and wax, windows, post-construction
- Square footage by floor type and restroom count, which drive hours more than total area
- Turnover and training time for new staff on the account

## Remodeling

- Demolition and haul-away *
- Protection of the rest of the house *
- Allowances for owner-selected items (tile, fixtures, cabinets), with what happens if they go over *
- Supervision and project management time *
- Final clean *
- Hidden-condition contingency (rot, out-of-plumb framing, outdated wiring)
- Design and drawings, engineering, HOA approval
- Temporary facilities: portable toilet, temporary kitchen, storage container

## General contracting

- Supervision and project management *
- General conditions: temporary power, water, toilets, fencing, signage *
- Insurance and bonds required by the contract *
- Allowances *
- Punch list and closeout documents *
- Sub bids leveled against the same scope before markup
- Schedule-driven costs: winter conditions, overtime, weekend work
- Retainage, and the cost of waiting for it

## IT and AV installs

- Cable pathways: conduit, J-hooks, firestopping, core drilling *
- Configuration and programming time *
- Testing and certification of cable runs *
- Labeling and as-built documentation *
- Training and handover session *
- Mounting hardware, brackets, backing *
- Licenses and subscriptions the client must renew
- After-hours work for occupied offices; ceiling access permits in multi-tenant buildings
- Rack, power, and cooling capacity at the head end
