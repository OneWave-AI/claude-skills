# Terms, Change Orders, Deposits, and Exclusions

**Not legal advice.** Contract terms, deposit limits, cancellation rights, lien notices, and license disclosures are set by state, provincial, and national law and change over time. Use this file to draft plain-language terms, then have the business's own attorney review the template before it is used with clients. When the user asks whether a term is enforceable or required, tell them to ask their attorney.

## Plain-language terms to cover

- **What is included:** the line items, the option the client selects, and any add-ons they initial.
- **What is not included:** the exclusions list. Anything not listed is not included.
- **Validity:** the price holds until a stated date. Short windows protect against material price changes.
- **Change orders:** changes to scope are priced and approved in writing (signature, or email reply if the contract allows) before the work is done. State how hidden conditions are handled: stop, document with photos, price, and get approval.
- **Payment schedule:** deposit, progress milestones tied to visible events (equipment set, rough inspection passed), final on completion. Tie each payment to something the client can see.
- **Late payment:** due dates, and any late fee the business chooses, subject to local law.
- **Allowances:** the dollar amount included for client-selected items, and that overages are billed as a change.
- **Warranty:** workmanship period, what it covers, what voids it; manufacturer warranties pass through separately.
- **Schedule and delays:** start window, what the client must provide (access, selections, cleared rooms), and that delays outside the business's control extend the schedule.
- **Site conditions:** utilities available, parking, working hours, pets, occupied spaces.
- **Cancellation:** how either side cancels and what is owed for work done and materials ordered.

## Deposits and cancellation rights: check before you draft

- Some jurisdictions cap deposits on home-improvement contracts or require specific wording. Check the contractor licensing board where the job is.
- In the US, the FTC Cooling-Off Rule gives buyers three business days to cancel certain sales made at their home or at temporary locations: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buyers-remorse-ftcs-cooling-rule-may-help. Some states add their own home-solicitation rules.
- Mechanics lien rights often depend on preliminary notices sent within deadlines. Ask the attorney which notices the business must send.
- Work on US housing built before 1978 that disturbs painted surfaces falls under the EPA Renovation, Repair and Painting rule, which requires certified firms and a pre-renovation pamphlet: https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program

## Change order template (plain language)

```
Change Order #___ to Quote #___            Date: ______
What changed: ______________________________________
Why: [client request / hidden condition found / code requirement]
Price change: +$______ (or credit -$______)
Schedule change: +___ days
New contract total: $______
Approved by client: ____________   Date: ______
Approved by contractor: ____________   Date: ______
```

## Exclusion wording that prevents disputes

Write exclusions the client can check against the site, not boilerplate. Examples:

- Drywall, paint, or tile repair beyond the openings needed for this work.
- Correction of existing code violations not caused by this work, unless the inspector requires it (priced as a change order).
- Hazardous materials (asbestos, lead, mold) testing or abatement.
- Structural repairs, engineering, or drawings.
- Work on items the client supplies, and warranty on those items.
- Utility company fees or upgrades on the utility side of the meter.
- Permit: none required for this scope (say so when true, so the client knows it was considered).

## Assumption wording

Assumptions state what the price depends on. When one turns out false, it becomes a change order instead of an argument.

- Normal working hours, one mobilization, clear access to the work area.
- Existing [ducts / wiring / framing] are in serviceable condition.
- Quantities based on [site measurements on date / drawings dated]; field-verified quantities that differ are billed at the quoted unit prices.
